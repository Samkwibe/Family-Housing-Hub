## 🚀 HOW TO USE IN-APP NAVIGATION

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Open Nearby Places**
   - Click "Nearby Places" in sidebar
   - Allow location access

2. **Find a Destination**
   - Search for a place OR
   - Browse categories OR
   - Click on a marker on map

3. **Start Navigation**
   - Click on a place card
   - Click "Start Navigation" button
   - Route appears instantly!

4. **Follow the Route**
   - Blue line shows your route
   - Top overlay shows distance & ETA
   - Map automatically centers on route
   - Follow the blue line!

5. **End Navigation**
   - Click "End" button when you arrive
   - Route disappears
   - Back to exploration mode
