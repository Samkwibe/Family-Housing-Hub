## 🎨 DESIGN TIPS

### Color Scheme:
- Use your app's color scheme (check your CSS/Tailwind config)
- Keep it consistent across slides
- Use high contrast for readability

### Fonts:
- Use clear, readable fonts (Arial, Calibri, or your app's font)
- Title: 44-48pt
- Body text: 24-32pt
- Keep text minimal - don't overcrowd slides

### Layout:
- One main idea per slide
- Use bullet points, not paragraphs
- Leave white space
- Align images and text consistently

---

## 💬 PRESENTATION TIPS

1. **Practice:** Read through your notes 2-3 times
2. **Timing:** Aim for 1-2 minutes per slide (7-14 minutes total)
3. **Eye Contact:** Look at your audience, not just the screen
4. **Pause:** Give time for questions between slides
5. **Enthusiasm:** Show you're excited about your project!
