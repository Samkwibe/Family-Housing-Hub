## 🎨 DESIGN TEMPLATE

### Color Scheme:
- **Primary:** Blue (#3B82F6) - Trust, technology
- **Secondary:** Green (#10B981) - Growth, success
- **Accent:** Orange (#F59E0B) - Energy, action
- **Background:** White or light gray

### Font Sizes:
- **Title:** 44-48pt (Bold)
- **Subtitle:** 32-36pt
- **Body Text:** 24-28pt
- **Small Text:** 18-20pt

### Layout Tips:
- Use consistent margins (1 inch on all sides)
- Align text left (easier to read)
- Use bullet points, not paragraphs
- One main idea per slide
- Leave white space (don't overcrowd)

## 💡 PRESENTATION TIPS

1. **Practice:** Read through 2-3 times before presenting
2. **Timing:** 1-2 minutes per slide (7-14 minutes total)
3. **Eye Contact:** Look at audience, not just screen
4. **Pause:** Give time for questions
5. **Be Enthusiastic:** Show you're excited about your project!
6. **Prepare for Questions:**
   - "How long did it take?" → "X weeks/months"
   - "What was hardest?" → "Integrating all the features"
   - "Will you continue?" → "Yes, adding more features"
